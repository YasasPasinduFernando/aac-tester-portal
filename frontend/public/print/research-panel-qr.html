Research panel QR passes

One A4 sheet. Cut down the middle. Laminate each half. Both codes open https://aac.yasaboy.com/.

  1. Print colour on A4, actual size, no fit-to-page.
  2. Cut along the dashed line to make two passes.
  3. Laminate each half in a pouch (about 125–250 micron), then trim a thin border.
